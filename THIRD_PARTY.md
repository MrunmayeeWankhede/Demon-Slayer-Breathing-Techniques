# Third-party components

The package contains original controller, builder, UI, and procedural shader source. It does not include anime images, footage, or audio. Demon Slayer and the referenced breathing/form names belong to their respective rights holders. The custom hand gestures and visuals are an unofficial fan-project interpretation.

The launcher obtains these separately:

- [MediaPipe](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe) and the official Hand Landmarker model from Google.
- [Vosk](https://github.com/alphacep/vosk-api) and [vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15](https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models).
- [OpenCV](https://github.com/opencv/opencv-python), [NumPy](https://numpy.org/), [python-osc](https://github.com/attwad/python-osc), and [sounddevice](https://github.com/spatialaudio/python-sounddevice).

These dependencies and downloaded models retain their respective upstream licenses. Their source and license details are available at the linked projects. TouchDesigner is separately installed software from Derivative and requires an appropriate license for your use.
